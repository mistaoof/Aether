§1Foundations

The layered-field
picture

Aether is not the substance the four forces are made from. It is a layer beneath them — coupled to all four, sourced by none, and not observable except through that coupling.

A caster works entirely at the lower surface. Every visible effect — heat, light, lift, curvature — is what the upper surface does once a ripple reaches it.

Picture two surfaces of water, one directly above the other, connected only by narrow, invisible channels rather than by direct contact. The upper surface is the one anyone can see: the four fundamental fields, doing what fields do, indifferent to whatever lies beneath them. The lower surface is aether.

Nothing about the lower surface is visible from above unless something disturbs it. But a disturbance sourced correctly, finding one of those channels, arrives at the upper surface as a real physical distortion of the field that channel connects to. A person able to perceive and address the aether field is not casting a spell in the traditional sense — they are disturbing a field beneath the ones matter and radiation actually experience, and using that disturbance's own onward spread to push the upper fields into a configuration of their choosing.

Eq. 1.1 — Aether Field Decomposition Ae(x, t) = Ae_0 + dAe(x, t)
§2Core philosophy

Three axes, and
not one of them is mana

There is no resource pool. No stamina meter, no supply of aether to run out of. What a practitioner can do is set by three independent quantities — moving along one does nothing to the other two.

Comprehension

The ceiling

Exactly the union of coupling channels a practitioner has a proven functional form for — nothing more generous, nothing less. It does not vary from casting to casting. It is earned one way only: by solving mathematics that was not solved before.

dM — the comprehension boundary
§3.5 · Eq. 3.2

Fidelity

Sourcing quality

How coherently a given casting's ripple reproduces the ideal structure for a channel already comprehended. It varies casting to casting, hour to hour, and is exactly as sensitive to injury, distraction, and rush as you would expect a fine motor skill to be.

Fid — per-casting, 0 < Fid < 1
§3.6 · Eq. 3.4

Practice depth

Medium independence

Whether one specific channel has been drilled past the threshold at which it can be sourced through visualization alone — no drawn glyph, no spoken cadence, nothing external to stabilize against. Earned per technique, never in general.

prac ≥ prac_min — per technique

Sim[…] is not a fourth axis. Simulated invocation (§3.7) is a zero-risk training method that accelerates fidelity and practice depth without ever touching comprehension. What it grants is a caster who reaches their existing ceiling more reliably — never a caster whose ceiling has moved.

§3.3Who can do what

The Power Hierarchy

Five tiers mark qualitative jumps in comprehension — a whole term of the Grand Equation opened up. Between the first three of those sits a named subclass: a rung defined not by a new term, but by an incomplete version of the next one. Tier sets the ceiling, never the outcome of any single casting.

delta(F_f) — gauge sector delta(M_op) — quark sector delta(g) — metric sector unreached
The Unsolved Ceiling§3.4 · not a tier

The full A_op integral — total, unconditional reality rewriting. It is occupied by no one and cannot be: the complete equation admits no closed-form solution by any finite mind. It is the limit the whole ladder points at without reaching, and it is what keeps power in this system from ever being a matter of permission.

A_op — no closed form exists for any finite dM

§3.3Beyond Legend

Four paths toward
divinity. None of them arrive.

Legend is where the one-term-at-a-time discipline runs out of room. What lies past it is not a ninth tier but a fork — four historically attested directions, each reaching for something the Grand Equation was never decomposed to make easy. None has ever been completed. None ever can be. A character can spend several lifetimes closing the distance, and the distance does not reach zero.

Path I · Eq. 4.24

The Tetrarch

Gauge unification — the whole channel at once

One coupling in place of all four. A caster near this limit no longer chooses which force an effect expresses through: fire, gravity, and the strong force become facets of one coupling rather than four separate ones. It never touches matter or geometry.

L_gauge -> k_unified * Tr(F_unified . F_unified) no closed form for k_unified is known Never completed

The Demiurge

Matter generation — the rule, not the list

Past a Master's full diagonalization toward the generating structure of M_op itself — not a complete list of solved eigenvectors but the rule that produces them, letting a caster originate stable matter never observed before. The most overtly creator-god path, and the most narrowly bounded.

Path III · Eq. 4.26

The Cosmographer

Unbounded geometry — past where Legend stopped

Legend's own trajectory carried further: terrain, climate, and the causal structure of a region reshaped as a permanent fact of geography rather than a held effect. The limit is the Unsolved Ceiling wearing a single term's clothing.

Comprehension itself — the boundary, not a term

The only path that targets no term of L_total at all. Several practitioners' proven comprehension domains merged into one acting boundary — fastest to dramatic gains, and the most explicitly horrifying in every tradition that has attempted it, since what is merged is not knowledge alone but the minds that held it.
